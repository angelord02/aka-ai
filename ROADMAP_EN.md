# Project Roadmap: Industrial AI Blockchain

## Phase 1: Foundation & Infrastructure (Months 1-4)
- **Blockchain Layer**: Development of the core ledger and smart contracts for data provenance.
- **Prototype AI Modules**: Initial training skeletons for Alpha AI, AKA MINING, and AKA ECO.
- **Hardware Strategy**: Planning for renewable-energy-powered data centers in key African regions.

## Phase 2: APK Ecosystem & Data Ingestion (Months 5-8)
- **APK Framework**: Release of the SDK for specialized industrial applications.
- **Data Agents**: Deployment of autonomous agents for big data collection and filtering.
- **Initial Training**: Baseline model training on the first wave of collected industrial data.

## Phase 3: Evolutionary Integration (Months 9-12)
- **M2N2 Implementation**: Applying evolutionary model merging to unify specialized niches.
- **Optimization**: Implementing DeepSeek-inspired memory and energy optimization techniques.
- **Beta Testing**: Internal rollout of Alpha AI and AKA MINING for early adopters.

## Phase 4: Public Deployment & Scaling (Months 13-18)
- **Public AI Model**: Release of a general-purpose model derived from the industrial platform.
- **Global Expansion**: Integration of AKA ECO with international DeFi protocols.
- **Maintenance & Security**: Continuous auditing and predictive maintenance of the decentralized network.

## Ongoing: Security & Maintenance
- Real-time monitoring for data integrity.
- Regular updates to the LLM core using fresh industrial data.
- Portability studies to ensure the APK ecosystem remains cross-platform.
